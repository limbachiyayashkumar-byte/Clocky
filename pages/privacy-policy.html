Privacy Policy

How Clocky handles your information

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Glitgrace (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with the Clocky mobile application and related services (collectively, the “Service”). By using Clocky, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information (such as email address, name) if you create an account
  • Support communications when you contact us at support@glitgrace.in
  • Feedback or survey responses you choose to submit

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • App usage data (e.g., feature interactions) in anonymized or aggregated form where possible
  • Diagnostic and crash data to improve stability

2.3 Alarm & Schedule Data

Alarm times, labels, and preferences are primarily stored on your device. If any such data is synced to our servers (for backup or multi-device features), it is treated as personal data and protected accordingly.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Clocky app
  • Process account-related requests (including deletion)
  • Respond to support inquiries
  • Send important service notices (not marketing spam)
  • Analyze usage in aggregated form to improve features
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these limited cases:

  • With service providers who assist us in operating the Service (under confidentiality obligations)
  • When required by law or to protect our rights and users’ safety
  • In connection with a business transfer (e.g., merger), subject to appropriate protections
  • With your consent

5. Data Storage & Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your device and account credentials secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. When you request account deletion, we delete or anonymize your data as described on our Account Deletion page.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability (where applicable)

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@glitgrace.in.

8. Children’s Privacy

Clocky is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services (e.g., analytics, crash reporting, payment processors via app stores). These services have their own privacy policies. Purchases are handled by Apple, Google, or other stores according to their terms.

10. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place where required by law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change accordingly. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

Privacy & Data Requests:
Email: support@glitgrace.in
