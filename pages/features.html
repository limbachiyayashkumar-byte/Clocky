Powerful Features

Everything you need for smarter, better mornings

⏰

Multiple Smart Alarms

Create unlimited alarms with custom labels, unique sounds, and flexible repeat options (daily, weekdays, weekends, or custom days).

🌅

Gentle Wake-Up Mode

Volume and optional screen brightness gradually increase so you wake up naturally instead of being startled.

🔕

Intelligent Snooze

Set your preferred snooze length. Progressive volume on repeated snoozes helps prevent oversleeping.

📅

Schedules & Reminders

Plan one-time or recurring reminders for meetings, medication, workouts, and daily routines.

🎵

Rich Sound Library

Calm nature sounds, energetic tones, and the option to use your own music files as alarm sounds.

🌙

Sleep Tracking Insights

Optional sleep duration logging so you can see patterns and improve your rest over time.

🔒

Privacy Focused

Alarm data and preferences stay on your device. We do not sell or share your personal information.

🎨

Beautiful Themes

Light and dark modes with elegant interfaces designed for clarity at a glance, even half-awake.

⚡

Reliable & Lightweight

Optimized to run efficiently and deliver alarms on time even when the app is in the background.
