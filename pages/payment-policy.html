Payment Policy

How purchases and billing work for Clocky

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Overview

This Payment Policy explains how payments are processed for the Clocky mobile application and any related digital products or subscriptions offered by Glitgrace (“we”, “us”, or “our”). By making a purchase, you agree to this policy and our Terms of Service.

2. Pricing

Prices for Clocky premium features, subscriptions, or one-time purchases are displayed in the app store (Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or other authorized platforms) or within the app at the time of purchase. Prices may vary by region, currency, and applicable taxes.

We reserve the right to change prices at any time. Price changes for existing subscriptions will be communicated according to the rules of the relevant app store and applicable law.

3. Payment Methods

Payments are processed exclusively through the official app store or payment platform from which you download or subscribe to Clocky (for example, Apple, Google, or other authorized partners). We do not directly collect or store your full credit card or banking details.

  • Payment is charged to your app store account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Subscriptions may renew automatically unless cancelled according to the store’s instructions.
  • You are responsible for ensuring that your payment method is valid and that sufficient funds are available.

4. Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription:

  • Your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each billing period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the renewal date (or as required by the app store).
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID, Google Play account, or the relevant store settings.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not typically refund the current period already paid (see our Refund Policy).

5. Taxes

Applicable sales tax, VAT, GST, or similar taxes may be added to the price depending on your location and the rules of the app store. Tax amounts are calculated and collected by the store or payment processor.

6. Failed Payments

If a payment fails (e.g., expired card, insufficient funds), the app store may retry the charge or suspend access to paid features until payment succeeds. We are not responsible for interruptions caused by payment failures on your account.

7. Billing Support

For questions about charges, invoices, or payment status:

Email: support@glitgrace.in

Please include your app store account email, approximate purchase date, and order or transaction ID if available.

Many billing issues (especially refunds and subscription cancellations) must be handled directly through Apple or Google according to their policies. We will guide you when that is the case.

8. Unauthorized Charges

If you believe a charge was made in error or without authorization, contact us immediately at support@glitgrace.in and also report the issue to your app store and, if needed, your bank or card issuer.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Payment Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of paid features after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any payment-related questions:

Support Email: support@glitgrace.in
