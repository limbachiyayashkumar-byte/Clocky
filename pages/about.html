About Clocky

Helping you start every day with intention

Our Mission

Clocky was built with a simple goal: make waking up less stressful and more reliable. We believe the first moments of your day set the tone for everything that follows.

Whether you need a gentle sunrise-style wake-up, multiple alarms for a busy schedule, or smart reminders that keep you on track, Clocky is designed to be the dependable companion by your bedside.

Clocky is developed and operated by Glitgrace. We focus on privacy, reliability, and a clean user experience so you can trust the app every single morning.

Who We Are

Glitgrace is the team behind Clocky. We build digital products that respect users and deliver real everyday value. Our support team is available at support@glitgrace.in for any questions, feedback, or account-related requests.

Our Values

  • User first — Features are designed around real morning routines, not trends.
  • Privacy — We minimize data collection and keep your alarm and schedule data under your control.
  • Reliability — An alarm app must work when it matters. We prioritize stability and timely notifications.
  • Transparency — Clear policies on payments, refunds, and account deletion so you always know where you stand.
